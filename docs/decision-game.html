How it works

Read the whole sentence

The picture, the middle box and the word make one sentence: [picture] is not Cow. Answer True if the sentence is right. The “is not” is where most mistakes happen.

Four ways to play

Practice: warm up as long as you like; nothing is recorded. Short and Long: 10 or 20 questions, ranked by fewest mistakes, then fastest time. Endless: keep going until your first mistake. You get 1 second per answer, 0.1 s less every 50 correct, down to 0.3 s.

Why it helps

Each answer asks you to see, read, hold a rule in mind and choose, quickly and without guessing. Short daily rounds build the habit of deciding clearly under a little pressure, and your scores show the progress.
