Why play

Each game asks for something we use all day: noticing, remembering, and choosing well under a little pressure. A few minutes of practice, every day, is a good habit, and it's fun to see your own numbers move.

We make no medical claims: these are games for practice and enjoyment, not treatments. Play for the challenge, the streak, and the friendly competition.
