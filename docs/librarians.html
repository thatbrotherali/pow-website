For readers

Ask your library to stock these books

Libraries buy the books their members ask for. One request from you can put a book on the shelf for everyone in your town, free to borrow.

  1. Find “Suggest a purchase”It's on most library websites, often under Services or your account, or just ask at the desk.
  2. Give the title, author and ISBNThe ISBNs are listed above. Ask for the paperback if you're not sure.
  3. Use the library apps tooIf your library uses Libby, hoopla or BorrowBox, look for the books there once the library ebook editions are out, and tap Notify me or Suggest.
