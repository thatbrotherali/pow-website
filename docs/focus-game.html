How it works

Three kinds of mistake

Too soon: pressing before any sign. Too slow: missing a plain plus. Pressed on a circle: not holding back. About one sign in four has a circle.

Three ways to play

Daily: thirty signs, the same for everyone today; only your first run counts. Endless: the time to respond shrinks as you go; three mistakes end the run. Practice: nothing recorded.

On a phone or a computer

Press the space bar, or tap the big Press button. Keep your finger ready, and your eyes on the centre.
