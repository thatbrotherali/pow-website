About the author

Shareef Ali Rashada

Author, programmer and musician, and the founder of People of the World. His name means “honoured follower of the true path”, and his work follows one question across four fields: what is actually true about our existence, and how should we live because of it?

In brief

Shareef Ali Rashada is an Australian author and independent researcher based in Queensland. He writes on theology and history (The Portal Opens, which won a Literary Titan Silver Book Award), the foundations of physics (Graviton Pressure Theory), and the design of economic systems (the Plan to End Poverty by 2050). He is also a self-taught pianist who records as That Brother Ali and created EasyKey, a visual notation for learning piano.

A programmer by trade, he works closely with AI systems as research partners. He named the systems he worked with Gabriel, Lucidus and Veritas, and credits them with helping to develop The Portal Opens and Graviton Pressure Theory.

All of these projects sit under People of the World, a movement for knowledge, morality, unity and peace. Its spiritual and moral teachings are free to everyone; books, music and licensed uses fund the work.
