Appendix C: Implementation Logistics and Pilot Community Simulation Methodology

Purpose This appendix defines the operational methodology for validating the People of the World framework in a real community environment. It provides a logistics model, instrumentation plan, and evaluation pathway for measuring how the integration of Civic Credits (CC), Poverty Elimination Centers (PECs), and LifeLink systems affects economic inclusion, material flows, and service delivery during the first two years of implementation.

Pilot Community Design Selection Criteria

  • Population: 50 000–75 000 residents (medium town or district capital).
  • Socio-economic profile: mixed informal and formal labor, poverty headcount > 30 %.
  • Infrastructure baseline: reliable grid ≥70 %, mobile coverage ≥90 %, existing schools ≥4, health posts ≥2.
  • Governance readiness: local PECA office established; legal recognition of Civic Credit Act and LifeLink Act.

Sampling and Control Groups

  • 5 000 households enrolled in LifeLink pilot (treatment).
  • 2 000 households maintained as control (standard government services only).
  • Statistical power ≥0.9 for detecting 10 % income change at p < 0.05.

Appendix H: Institutional Charters and Legal Templates

Purpose Appendix H consolidates the foundational legal and constitutional templates for the governing institutions of the People of the World Poverty Elimination Framework. Each charter is drafted to a standard that allows immediate adaptation by national legislatures or treaty bodies while maintaining global interoperability and ethical integrity.

H.1 Poverty Elimination and Civic Authority (PECA) Charter Preamble Recognizing the right of every human being to capability and dignity, the State hereby establishes the Poverty Elimination and Civic Authority (PECA) as a permanent civic institution mandated to coordinate and regulate the national implementation of the LifeLink and Civic Credit systems.

Articles of Foundation 1. Legal Status: PECA shall be constituted as an independent statutory authority answerable to Parliament through the Ministry of Human Capability.

2. Mandate:
