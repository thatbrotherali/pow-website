The Plan to End Poverty

A Unified Framework for Human Capability and Economic Renewal · 2025 edition

A designed system to end poverty by 2050, built on three instruments: Civic Credits, which pay for verified service to the community; Poverty Elimination Centers, which bring housing, care, education and work under one roof; and LifeLink, which connects every citizen to public services.
