### 2. $\kappa$ — Coherence Coupling Ratio

### 3. $T_{gp}$ — Graviton Torque

**Units:** $$= gp \cdot \text{length} = \frac{\text{kg} \cdot \text{m}^2}{\text{s}^2}$$

- $T_{gp} > 0$: spin faster than field— decelerative torque applied
