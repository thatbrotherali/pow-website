**Definition:**

**Symbol Use:**

### 1. *Cres* — Coherence Resistance

**Definition:** *Cres* is a measure of a structure’s internal field resilience— how well its graviton-coherent lattice resists external compression by graviton inflow.

**Units:** <span class="math display">\\= \frac{gp}{\text{volume}} = \frac{\text{kg}}{\text{m}^4 \cdot \text{s}^2}\\</span>

**Interpretation:**

- High *Cres* means the object retains its form even under intense external field pressure.

- Low *Cres* structures deform, collapse, or reconfigure.

- Analogous to elastic modulus in materials science, but causally tied to pressure-driven coherence.

**Field-Density Formula:** <span class="math display">\\\textit{Cres} = \frac{gp\_{ext}}{\rho\_{coh}} \quad \text{where} \\ \rho\_{coh} = \text{internal coherence density}\\</span>

**Applications:**

- Determining structural integrity under stellar or orbital compression.

- Modeling resistance to collapse in gravitational lensing zones.

- Mapping coherent durability in biological systems or field technology.

### 2. <span class="math inline">\\\kappa\\</span> — Coherence Coupling Ratio

**Definition:** <span class="math inline">\\\kappa\\</span> (kappa) is the ratio between internal spin coherence and external orbital field resonance.

**Units:** Dimensionless

**Formula:** <span class="math display">\\\kappa = \frac{L_s}{L_o} = \frac{I \cdot \omega}{mvr}\\</span> Where:

- <span class="math inline">\\L_s\\</span>: spin angular momentum (<span class="math inline">\\I \cdot \omega\\</span>)

- <span class="math inline">\\L_o\\</span>: orbital angular momentum (<span class="math inline">\\mvr\\</span>)

- <span class="math inline">\\I\\</span>: moment of inertia

- <span class="math inline">\\\omega\\</span>: angular spin rate

- <span class="math inline">\\m, v, r\\</span>: orbital mass, tangential velocity, and radius respectively

**Interpretation:**

- <span class="math inline">\\\kappa \approx 1\\</span>: system is field-resonant and dynamically stable.

- <span class="math inline">\\\kappa \ll 1\\</span>: spin is negligible— object more influenced by external field.

- <span class="math inline">\\\kappa \gg 1\\</span>: excessive internal spin— may lead to destabilization or precessional bleed-off.

**Applications:**

- Tidal locking prediction

- Spin-orbit resonance detection

- Field alignment diagnostics

### 3. <span class="math inline">\\T\_{gp}\\</span> — Graviton Torque

**Definition:** <span class="math inline">\\T\_{gp}\\</span> quantifies the restorative torque exerted on a rotating object due to misalignment with the local graviton field’s rotational corridor <a href="#fn5" id="fnref5" class="footnote-ref" role="doc-noteref"><sup>5</sup></a>.

**Units:** <span class="math display">\\= gp \cdot \text{volume} = \frac{\text{kg} \cdot \text{m}^2}{\text{s}^2}\\</span>

**Formula:** <span class="math display">\\T\_{gp} = -\gamma (\Omega - \Omega\_{corr})\\</span> Where:

- <span class="math inline">\\\Omega\\</span>: actual spin angular velocity of the object

- <span class="math inline">\\\Omega\_{corr}\\</span>: preferred resonance angular velocity of local field

- <span class="math inline">\\\gamma\\</span>: graviton coupling constant (system dependent)

**Interpretation:**

- <span class="math inline">\\T\_{gp} \> 0\\</span>: spin faster than field— decelerative torque applied

- <span class="math inline">\\T\_{gp} \< 0\\</span>: spin slower than field— accelerative torque applied

- <span class="math inline">\\T\_{gp} = 0\\</span>: resonance lock achieved

**Applications:**

- Axial precession modeling

- Ring structure formation

- Magnetosphere decay or restoration torque

### 4. <span class="math inline">\\R\_{field}\\</span> — Resonance Feedback Coefficient

**Definition:** <span class="math inline">\\R\_{field}\\</span> measures how much an object’s motion constructively amplifies or destructively disrupts the surrounding graviton field.

**Units:** <span class="math display">\\= gp \cdot \text{time} \cdot \text{volume} = \frac{\text{kg} \cdot \text{m}^2}{\text{s}}\\</span>

**Interpretation:**

- High <span class="math inline">\\R\_{field}\\</span>: object reinforces field structure— constructive resonance

**Applications:**
