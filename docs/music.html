Where the music comes from

In his early twenties, Shareef had a friend who could play a cheap little keyboard better than much of what was on the radio, without reading a note. That friend kept pushing him to try. Shareef taught himself from library books, found traditional notation hard going, and kept playing anyway.

The music came first. The same struggle later produced EasyKey, a way of writing music that shows exactly which keys to press, so anyone can learn to play.
