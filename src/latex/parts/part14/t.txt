Introduction
What precedes this point has been a necessary reckoning.
We have examined abstraction, challenged orthodoxy, and exposed the conceptual shortcuts
embedded in the gravitational theories that shaped the last century. Each part has pulled
back a curtain: not to dismiss the insights of Newton or Einstein, but to show the gaps their
successors left open and the questions they left unanswered.
But deconstruction is not enough. This is the turning point. We now move into the second half
of the framework: the foundation, articulation and expansion of Graviton Pressure Theory.
GPT does not reject what has been measured. Every number that Newton and Einstein got
right, it must get right too. What it restores is the thing the numbers were never given:
• It restores force where geometry obscured it.
• It restores directionality where equations grew silent.
• It restores coherence where abstraction unravelled.
• And above all, it restores causality, where physics lost its voice.

14.1

What the First Half Established

The first half of the framework makes one argument, from many sides: description is not
mechanism. An equation can predict an outcome to many decimal places and still say nothing
about what touches what, or what is exchanged. The test applied throughout, to the mainstream’s explanations and to GPT’s own, is the interface test: name the interface, or mark the

• Description took the place of cause (Part 2). Modern science traded explanation for
description, and left no room for mind, meaning or purpose.2
• The explanations do not agree (Part 3). Every gravitational phenomenon has a Newtonian account, a geometric account, and often a frame-force or energy account; each
borrows from the others where its own runs out. Matching numbers are not matching

mechanism should be.12
• The Moon and the tides (Part 13). The tides, both bulges, follow from one located

14.3

What the Model Reproduces

A new account of gravity must first give back everything that is measured. GPT reproduces
the following, each with a named mechanism: [Argued]
• Free fall is the same for every body, to one part in 1015 , because momentum is delivered
in proportion to mass.14
• A scale reads mg, because it measures the current of momentum relayed through the
held body; an accelerometer reads zero in free fall, because the delivery is uniform.
• Orbits, because a body coasting through a medium that is itself accelerating inward
follows the Newtonian path; and the advance of Mercury’s perihelion, 43′′ per century,
in the river form.
• Clocks: the slowing of clocks in gravity and in motion, as one effect, to the precision the
GPS system requires, and the lengthened lives of fast muons.
• Light: bending by 1.75′′ at the Sun, verified by tracing rays through the flowing medium,
and the delay of signals passing the Sun.
• Tides: both bulges, the Sun’s share, the solid-Earth tide, and the slowing of the Earth’s
spin as the Moon recedes.
These are not small matters. They are the tests that every theory of gravity since Newton has
had to pass, and GPT passes them with a cause at each step.

A theory that only reproduces is a translation. GPT makes predictions that the standard
accounts do not.
The relaxation boundary. Galaxies rotate as though held by more matter than they contain,
and the departure from Newton follows a single acceleration, a0 ≈ 1.2 × 10−10 metres per
second squared, across many
√ galaxies. GPT places a boundary where each mass’s inflow falls
to that acceleration, at r = GM/a0 : about 7,000 astronomical units from the Sun, far beyond

flattens. [Hypothesis]
One constant of the medium replaces a separate fit for every galaxy. This is GPT’s
central distinctive prediction, developed in Parts 9 and 16 and tested in Part 29.
The scale of the medium. The medium’s free-space pressure, P0 ≈ 4.5 × 10−10 pascals, is
numerically the pressure that would be set by the critical density of the universe, and of the
same size as the pressure attributed to dark energy. [Hypothesis]
The match is in magnitude only. In General
Relativity the pressure of dark energy is negative, and only a negative pressure accelerates

medium must drive the expansion by a different route, as a self-repelling medium that is not

Changes in the Second Edition
This list records what changed from the first edition (12 June 2025) and why. Changes to
the argument alter what the paper claims or how it supports a claim; clarifications improve
wording, sources or presentation and leave the argument as it was. The first edition’s purpose,
its voice and its closing lines are kept.

Changes to the argument
• The paper now states the model in one piece: a summary of the mechanism, a figure, and
a table of seventeen premises, each with its status and the part where it is developed.
• Four decisions are stated as premises (9 November 2026): rulers contract along the flow
(the river form), which gives the Schwarzschild solution exactly and Mercury’s 43′′ per
century; the medium’s scale is P0 ≈ 4.5 × 10−10 Pa, within the heat budget; the medium
carries stress as well as pressure, and so transverse waves; and the relaxation boundary
at a0 is GPT’s central distinctive prediction.
• The summary of the first half is corrected. The first edition said modern cosmology “has
survived not through precision, but through patchwork assumptions”; cosmology is
precise, and what it lacks is mechanism.
• The promise that the field “explains everything from planetary motion to time dilation,
galactic rotation to gravitational lensing, biological tides to quantum cohesion” is replaced
by three accounts: what the model reproduces, what it predicts that is its own, and what
it still owes.
• “GPT is not a speculative replacement” becomes “GPT does not reject what has been
measured”: much of GPT is a hypothesis, marked as one, and the claim it can defend is
that it restores cause.
• “Governs motion” is scoped to gravitational motion: living bodies move themselves
(Part 7).

Clarifications
• Premises table, rods (9 November): the ruler premise is given its mechanism, the medium
streaming through a rod’s bonds (Part 23), in place of the river form’s description alone.
• Premises table, clocks (9 November): a clock’s own rate is set by its structure; the factor
by which the medium slows it is common to every kind of clock.
• The subtitle changes from Transitioning into Graviton Pressure Theory to Graviton Pressure
Theory, Stated in One Piece.
• “Pressure corridors” and “coherent directional force” are not used here; corridors now
have their place inside matter (Part 21) and in magnets (Part 22).
• The road ahead is given by topic and follows the revised parts, with Part 19 (the pathway
to matter) inserted and Coherence and Corridors renumbered 20 and 21. The summary
of the first half now includes Parts 5 and 8 to 10.
• An “About This Paper” section, a figure, a table, status marks and this list are added.
