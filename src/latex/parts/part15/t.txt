Introduction

Conclusion
Gravitons in GPT are real: the constituents of a medium that fills space, moving, carrying
momentum, and oscillating in phase with one another. In phase, they push; out of phase,
they do not. From that rule come the medium’s pressure and the solidity of contact; with the
spatial phase of shared patterns, it gives the hold of a bond. Matter takes in the medium and
ejects it out of step; the active pressure falls toward every mass, and bodies are carried by the
medium’s acceleration. Far from each mass, the medium brings its ejecta back into step, and
gravity changes its form at the same acceleration around every mass.
With this, GPT moves gravitational theory from metaphor to mechanics, from inference to
interaction, and from passive geometry to a medium that does the work.
