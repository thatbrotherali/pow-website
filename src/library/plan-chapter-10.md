### Appendix H: Institutional Charters and Legal Templates

H.1 Poverty Elimination and Civic Authority (PECA) Charter Preamble Recognizing the right of every human being to capability and dignity, the State hereby establishes the Poverty Elimination and Civic Authority (PECA) as a permanent civic institution mandated to coordinate and regulate the national implementation of the LifeLink and Civic Credit systems.

Articles of Foundation 1. Legal Status: PECA shall be constituted as an independent statutory authority answerable to Parliament through the Ministry of Human Capability.

2\. Mandate:

\(a\) Operate and maintain the national network of Poverty Elimination Centers (PECs).

\(b\) Ensure transparent issuance and redemption of Civic Credits.

\(c\) Uphold ethical, fiscal, and operational standards consistent with ICCR protocols.

3\. Governance:

\(a\) Board of Governors (9 members) appointed for staggered 6-year terms.

\(b\) One-third of the Board elected by civic participants through LifeLink verified vote.

\(c\) Annual public audit of finances and decisions.

4\. Funding: Appropriations from the national treasury, service fees, and returns from PEC enterprise revenue.

5\. Accountability: Subject to independent audit by the LifeLink Ethics and Integrity Board (LEIB) and fiscal audit by the Civic Credit Authority (CCA).

H.2 Civic Credit Authority (CCA) Statutory Instrument Mandate The Civic Credit Authority (CCA) shall serve as the national monetary regulator for the Civic Credit economy, preserving fiscal stability, parity with national currency, and transparent audit of issuance and redemption.

Core Functions 1. Establish and manage the Civic Credit Stabilization Fund (CCSF).

2\. Authorize issuance ceilings and liquidity corridors.

3\. Oversee redemption of Civic Credits into national currency for institutional entities.

4\. Conduct quarterly audits of PECA financial ledgers.

5\. Publish an Annual Civic Monetary Report to Parliament and the ICCR.

Governance Structure

- Monetary Board of 7 Governors chaired by an appointee confirmed by both houses of Parliament.

Version Numbering Convention

- Major version (X.0): structural or policy-level change affecting compatibility.
- Minor version (X.Y): content expansion or non-breaking refinement.
- Revision (X.Y.Z): typographical, formatting, or cross-reference correction.

Archival Policy

- All superseded editions stored under immutable DOIs within the ICCR Archive Vault.
- Public citation must reference both version and DOI (e.g., POW-Framework v1.4 \[DOI:10.2040/pow.2025.v1.4\]).
- Annual verification ensures checksum integrity of archived PDFs and LaTeX sources.
