Project Title:

  • Poverty Elimination Centers (PECs)

Project Purpose and Justification:

  • To eradicate poverty by providing a comprehensive support system including education, vocational training, and healthcare services. The centers aim to empower individuals from diverse backgrounds to achieve economic independence and improve their life circumstances.

Objectives:

  • Establish self-sustaining campuses in capital cities across the USA and Australia, initially.
  • Provide educational and vocational training programs that cater to a diverse participant base.
  • Implement comprehensive health and wellness programs to support the physical and mental well-being of participants.
  • Foster community development and individual empowerment through structured programs and initiatives.

Scope:

  • Development of campuses equipped with educational facilities, vocational training centers, healthcare centers, residential units, and administrative offices.
  • Implementation of a curriculum and training programs across various disciplines.
  • Recruitment and training of staff to support educational, healthcare, and operational needs.

High-Level Requirements:

  • Acquisition of land in designated capital cities.
  • Construction of campus facilities adhering to sustainable practices.
  • Development of partnerships with educational institutions, healthcare providers, and government bodies.

Major Deliverables:

  • Construction of the first set of PEC campuses.
  • Launch of educational and vocational programs.
  • Operational healthcare facilities within the campuses.

Assumptions and Constraints:

  • Sufficient funding will be obtained from various sources, including government grants and private investments.
  • Regulatory approvals and compliance with local, state, and federal laws will be managed without significant delays.

Initial Stakeholders:

  • Government bodies, educational institutions, healthcare organizations, potential funders, and the local communities.

Project Sponsor:

  • Shareef Ali Rashada, Founder and Visionary

Preliminary Budget:

  • Estimated at $50 million for initial phase covering land acquisition, construction, and the first year of operations.

Project Timeline:

  • Initiation to completion of the first phase estimated to span approximately 18 months from the date of land acquisition.

Approval Requirements:

  • Formal approval from local government authorities for construction and operational permits.
  • Approval from financial stakeholders based on the feasibility and projected returns of the project.

Revision History:

  • Document creation and revisions to be logged with dates and descriptions of changes.
