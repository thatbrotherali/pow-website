Rethinking Mass

The Limits of Old Paradigms

In classical mechanics, mass is treated as a scalar quantity representing an object’s resistance to acceleration and gravitational force. Both models suffer from circular logic—force defines mass, and mass defines curvature—leaving causality undefined.
