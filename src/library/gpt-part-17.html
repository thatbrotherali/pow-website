Introduction

Mass is measured more precisely than almost anything in physics. A body’s resistance to being pushed and its weight agree, for every material tested, to one part in \(10^{15}\).5 A body’s mass rises with every joule of energy added to it and falls with every joule taken away, in the ratio \(E = mc^2\). These are among the best-established facts there are.

What mass is has no answer in the standard account. Newton defined it by the force needed to accelerate it; General Relativity makes it a source of spacetime curvature; the Standard Model gives each elementary particle a number for its coupling to a field that fills all space, measured and not derived. Each describes how mass behaves. None says what is there.

GPT’s first edition defined mass as a structure’s resistance to the flow of the medium. That instinct is kept: mass is a relation between a pattern and the medium it is made of. [Hypothesis] This edition says what the pattern is, what the relation is, and why the measured facts follow.

One principle guides it. The universe began hot and energetic, expanded and cooled, and everything in it came from that one beginning. So nothing can be made of anything else: each thing is a later phase or process of what came before, cooled energy settled into some form. The medium is the first such form; light is its travelling wave; matter is its knotted circulation; nuclei, atoms and everything after are made of the knots. “Fundamental” means earliest, not uncaused. How knots form, why they need three dimensions, and how they build nuclei and atoms is the subject of Part 19, The Pathway to Matter. This paper is about what confined energy does, and about its numbers.

What Mass Measures

Mass is measured in three ways, and they always agree. [Argued] Inertial mass is how hard a body is to accelerate. Passive gravitational mass is how strongly it is carried toward another mass; torsion balances and the MICROSCOPE satellite find it in the same ratio to inertial mass for every material, to one part in \(10^{15}\). Active gravitational mass is how strongly a body draws other bodies toward it; ranging to the Moon, whose iron core and rocky mantle would pull unequally if active and passive mass differed, finds them equal to about one part in \(10^{12}\).6

Mass is energy. [Argued] Every change in a body’s energy changes its mass by \(\Delta m = \Delta E/c^2\):

  • Heat. Warming a kilogram of water by 100 degrees adds 419 kilojoules and \(4.7\times10^{-12}\) kilograms.

  • Binding. A helium nucleus weighs 0.76 per cent less than the two protons and two neutrons it is made of; the difference is the energy released in binding them.

  • Motion. A body’s energy, and its resistance to further acceleration, rise as \(\gamma = 1/\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}\). Electrons at the LEP collider reached \(\gamma \approx 2\times10^5\).

  • Stored energy. A phone battery charged with 15 watt-hours gains \(6\times10^{-13}\) kilograms.

  • Phase changes. Freezing a kilogram of water releases 334 kilojoules; the ice has less mass by \(3.7\times10^{-12}\) kilograms.

The conservation of mass in chemistry is the conservation of energy, at energies too small for \(\Delta E/c^2\) to be weighed. Any account of mass must give all of this exactly.

Matter Is Energy Slowed Down

Hypothesis

Matter is motion of the medium at the speed of light, confined to a small region so that it goes nowhere. Its energy is the energy of that motion; its mass is that energy divided by \(c^2\); and its rest is an average over motion at \(c\).

The mainstream already contains most of the evidence for this, without drawing the conclusion.

A box of light has mass.

Light trapped between two mirrors carries energy \(E\) but, bouncing back and forth, goes nowhere. The box with the light in it weighs more, by \(E/c^2\), and it is harder to accelerate by the same amount. The reason is mechanical (Figure 1). Push the box, and the rear mirror moves into the light arriving at it, which strikes it shifted up in frequency and pushes harder; the front mirror moves away from the light arriving at it, which strikes shifted down and pushes less. The light resists the acceleration, and the resistance comes out exactly \(m = E/c^2\). Trapped motion at \(c\) behaves as mass, with inertia, from the imbalance of what it delivers to front and back. [Argued]

The arithmetic of the box.

Let the trapped light be two pulses, each of energy \(E/2\), running opposite ways. Move the box at speed \(v\), with \(\beta = v/c\). The pulse running forward is shifted up in frequency by the factor \(k = \sqrt{(1+\beta)/(1-\beta)}\), and the pulse running back is shifted down by \(1/k\). Their total energy and momentum are then \begin{equation}E_{\text{box}} = \frac{E}{2}\left(k + \frac1k\right) = \gamma E, \qquad p_{\text{box}} = \frac{E}{2c}\left(k - \frac1k\right) = \gamma\,\frac{E}{c^2}\,v , \label{eq:box}\end{equation} with \(\gamma = 1/\sqrt{1-\beta^2}\). That is exactly the energy and momentum of a body of mass \(m = E/c^2\) moving at \(v\). [Argued] Nothing was assumed about mass: the light had none. The factor \(k\) is the same one that sets the leading and trailing exchanges of a moving body in GPT (Part 23): a moving body’s momentum is the imbalance between its front and back. Pushing on Equation \(\eqref{eq:box}\) gives inertia its measured shape at high speed: a body resists a push along its motion as \(\gamma^3 m\) and across it as \(\gamma m\).

The proton is almost all confined motion.

The three quarks in a proton account, by their own masses, for about one per cent of its mass. The rest is the energy of the motion and binding confined inside it.7 A calculation from the theory of the strong force divides the proton’s mass roughly into a tenth from the quarks’ own masses and their interaction with the vacuum, about a third from the quarks’ motion, about a third from the energy of the field that binds them, and about a quarter from a quantum effect of that field.8 Most of the mass of everything that can be touched is confined energy. [Argued]

The electron moves at \(c\).

Dirac’s equation for the electron, one of the best-tested equations in physics, says that at every instant the electron’s velocity is \(\pm c\). What is seen as its motion is an average over a rapid trembling, which Schrödinger named Zitterbewegung: about \(10^{21}\) times a second over a distance near the electron’s Compton wavelength.9 It is too fast to observe in a free electron, but the same equation has been reproduced in trapped ions, and the trembling seen.10

An electron cannot be made smaller.

Trying to confine an electron within less than its Compton wavelength, \(2.4\times10^{-12}\) metres, does not produce a smaller electron. It produces new pairs of electrons and positrons.

Matter and light turn into each other.

An electron and a positron that meet become two flashes of light. Light with enough energy, passing a nucleus, becomes an electron and a positron;11 and in collisions of light with light alone, pairs of matter have been made from nothing but light.12 Matter is made of the same thing light is made of, in another form. [Argued] In GPT that thing is the medium: light is the medium’s travelling wave, and matter is its confined circulation.

The Turnover in Three Dimensions

Hypothesis

Matter is kept in being by a cycle that runs through it and out into space (Figure 3).

  1. Arrival. In-phase gravitons arrive at a knot from every direction, pressing. Around a lone knot the arrivals balance, and there is no net push. But the knot continually turns pressing medium into neutral medium, so the region around it holds slightly fewer pressing gravitons than the region far away. That deficit is a pressure gradient, and the medium drifts gently inward down it: the drift is the field, \(v^2 = 2GM/r\).

  2. Transfer. A graviton reaching the knot meets its phase boundary and is drawn into step with the circulation, as one oscillator locks onto a stronger one nearby. The self-repulsive energy it had from being in step with its surroundings goes into the circulation.

  3. Departure. It leaves carrying the knot’s phase: neutral to the surrounding pressure, but moving and carrying momentum. It travels out through the rest of the body, past its edges and into space, as the counterflow, carrying the pattern of the body’s spin and motion (Part 16).

  4. Return. Far out, the surrounding medium brings the neutral gravitons back into step. That re-phasing is the relaxation boundary (Part 15). In phase again, they press again, and are available to flow in again.

Matter is a turnover, not a lump, and matter and the medium are partners in one circulation. The pattern lasts while the medium passing through it changes, as a whirlpool or a flame keeps its shape while water or fuel passes through.

Why every electron is the same.

Every electron has the same mass, and so does every proton. On a picture in which each particle carries a stored amount from its birth, that is a coincidence of origin. On the turnover it follows: a knot’s energy is the balance between what it takes in and what it sheds for its winding, as every whirlpool in a given current settles to the same size. Why a body’s draw follows its energy.

But its turns per second are its energy divided by \(h\), so a fixed take per turn makes its intake proportional to its energy. In a body of many knots every circulation contributes; binding lowers the circulating energy, and the intake falls with it. So a body’s draw on the medium, its active gravitational mass, follows its total energy, binding included, [Argued] which is what the Moon’s ranging shows, and it puts the relaxation boundary at one acceleration, \(a_0\), around every mass (Part 15). Why matter is nearly transparent.

If each knot took most of what reached it, the outer knots of a body would starve the inner ones, and mass would depend on shape, as in Le Sage’s shadowing theory. Mass adds exactly, and gravitational shielding is bounded far below what such shadowing needs. So each knot takes only a tiny share of what passes, and the medium flows through matter almost freely, [Argued] as neutrinos pass through the Earth. Every knot in a body receives its share, and the body’s total is their sum.

What makes a particle an entity.

A knot’s material is replaced continually, so its identity cannot lie in its material. It lies in what the turnover cannot change: its winding (its charge and its baryon or lepton number), its handedness, its spin and its balance energy. [Hypothesis] These are exactly the quantities conserved in every interaction. A particle persists as a whirlpool does: as the invariants of a pattern the flow passes through.

Holding and supplying are different jobs.

The medium’s free-space pressure, \(P_0 \approx 4.5\times10^{-10}\) pascals, acting over an electron-sized area, gives a force of about \(10^{-35}\) newtons, some \(10^{34}\) times too small to hold an electron’s circulation on its loop. A second role for the counterflow.

The departing gravitons are neutral to the surrounding pressure, but they carry the knot’s phase. Passing through other knots, they meet a phase that is in step or out of step with it. The atom.

A hydrogen atom weighs less than a free proton and a free electron by its binding energy, 13.6 electronvolts: \(1.45\times10^{-8}\) of its mass. [Argued] Its numbers fit the circulation picture closely. Mass is energy, and matter is energy slowed down: motion of the medium at the speed of light, confined so that it goes nowhere. Light trapped in a box has exactly the energy and momentum of a mass \(E/c^2\); the proton is almost all confined motion; Dirac’s electron already contains a loop at \(c\) of radius \(\hbar/2mc\), turning \(2.5\times10^{20}\) times a second. The medium pressed into matter gives up its in-phase energy to the circulation and leaves as the neutral counterflow, to be re-phased far away and return: that turnover keeps matter in being, its balance gives every electron the same mass, and the inflow that supplies it is gravity. A pattern of the medium is carried by the medium, which is why every body falls alike; a fixed take per turn makes a body’s draw follow its energy, and the smallness of that take is why gravity is \(10^{36}\) times weaker than charge. Every change of energy changes mass by \(\Delta E/c^2\), from a charged battery to the binding of nuclei and the light of the Sun. The numbers of mass, the electron’s 0.511 MeV, the ratio 1836, the strength \(1/137\), are derived by no theory yet; they are GPT’s target.

Changes in the Second Edition

This list records what changed from the first edition and why. Changes to the argument alter what the paper claims or how it supports a claim; clarifications improve wording, sources or presentation and leave the argument as it was.

Changes to the argument

  • Mass is defined as the energy of a confined circulation of the medium at \(c\), divided by \(c^2\), in place of an impedance profile \(\rho_{\text{imp}} = f(\text{coherence, geometry, phase})\). The evidence that matter is confined motion is added: the box of light, the proton’s mass, Dirac’s electron, pairs from confinement, matter made from light.

  • The scale of that motion is set out: the electron’s loop, frequency, acceleration and force, and the conclusion that rest is the most extreme motion there is.

  • Inertia’s measured shape at high speed (\(\gamma\) across, \(\gamma^3\) along) is given. “No universal tendency to continue motion” is withdrawn; motion is kept as a relation with the medium.

  • The effects of heat, binding, stress, speed and melting on mass are kept, with their directions, and given their measured size, \(\Delta E/c^2\). Depth in a field is corrected: locally unchanged, reduced as seen from far away.

  • “Equal matter, different mass” and mass-tuning devices are restated: mass tracks energy to about \(10^{-12}\); changes beyond \(\Delta E/c^2\) are bounded and open. Effective mass in crystals is added as a measured case of inertia from exchange with a surrounding medium.

  • Photons are restated as the medium’s unconfined travelling wave. Neutrinos are given their measured mass. The classification table is replaced.

  • The turnover is added, joining the first edition’s “perpetually refreshed” mass and its absorption of gravitons, and set out as a full cycle in three dimensions, with its return through re-phasing, its energy route, every electron’s identical mass as a balance (tested by the constancy of the proton-to-electron mass ratio), a fixed take per turn giving a body’s draw in proportion to its energy, the transparency of matter, and identity as the invariants of the pattern.

  • Inertial, passive and active gravitational mass are separated. Passive mass follows from matter being made of the medium it falls in; an earlier wording of this edition, that a body is carried at a rate set by its own intake, is corrected.

  • New: the numbers of mass. The arithmetic of the box of light, giving \(\gamma E\) and \(\gamma (E/c^2) v\); the proton’s mass budget; a table of loop radius, turns, acceleration and holding force for five particles; the match between the loop and Dirac’s trembling; the magnetic moment and its correction; mass in the hydrogen atom and the meaning of \(1/137\) as the ratio of the electron’s orbital speed to its circulation; nuclear binding, with each term of the liquid-drop formula given a mechanism; mass and the stars; why gravity is weak; and a table of the numbers still to be derived, with Koide’s relation.

  • The formation of knots, their three dimensions, charge, the fragments of forced dissolution, and the path from knots to atoms, drafted for this paper, are moved to Part 19, The Pathway to Matter. Mass is how much; matter is how it is arranged.

  • The energy equation \(E = \int \Delta P_g\, dV\) and the reinterpretation of redshift through variable mass are withdrawn.
