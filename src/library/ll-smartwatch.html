Objective:

The LifeLink Smartwatch is a wearable health, security, and transaction hub, designed for biometric authentication, real-time health monitoring, AI-driven task management, and seamless Civic Credit transactions.

📌 Core Functions & Use Cases

Biometric Authentication Uses fingerprint, ECG, and skin conductivity sensors for ID verification. Secure login, payments, and health data encryption.
Health & Fitness Monitoring Tracks heart rate, oxygen levels, sleep patterns, and stress levels. AI-driven health coaching and PEC fitness programs.
AI-Powered Emergency Response Detects abnormal vitals, sudden falls, or medical emergencies. Automatically alerts emergency contacts or PEC health services.
Civic Credit Contactless Transactions NFC-enabled payments using LifeLink Civic Credits. Allows users to pay for goods/services at PECs and partner businesses.
Secure Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for LifeLink ID Acts as a secure digital key for user accounts. Strengthens identity verification for transactions, voting, and data access.
AI-Powered Task Management & Notifications Smart reminders for appointments, PEC training, work schedules, and financial tasks. LifeLink AI syncs with user schedules and productivity apps.
Encrypted Messaging & Voice Assistance AI-driven voice control and end-to-end encrypted communication. Used for confidential LifeLink communications and transactions.
Seamless Multi-Device Syncing Connects with Look Smart Glasses, Hear Buds, and LifeLink main unit. Unified LifeLink experience with cross-device support.

📌 Hardware & Connectivity

Display 1.8” AMOLED touchscreen, always-on display.
Sensors Heart rate, ECG, SpO2, skin conductivity, temperature, accelerometer, gyroscope.
Authentication Biometric fingerprint scanner & encrypted NFC chip.
Controls Touchscreen, voice commands, haptic feedback, rotating bezel.
Connectivity Bluetooth 5.3, Wi-Fi 6, 5G-ready, LifeLink SecureSync Protocol.
Battery Life 5-day standard use, 48-hour AI-intensive mode, wireless fast charging.
Water & Dust Resistance IP68-certified for dust/water resistance (up to 50m depth).
