# What Mass Measures

Mass is measured in three ways, and they always agree. **\[Argued\]** *Inertial* mass is how hard a body is to accelerate. *Passive gravitational* mass is how strongly it is carried toward another mass; torsion balances and the MICROSCOPE satellite find it in the same ratio to inertial mass for every material, to one part in $10^{15}$. *Active gravitational* mass is how strongly a body draws other bodies toward it; ranging to the Moon, whose iron core and rocky mantle would pull unequally if active and passive mass differed, finds them equal to about one part in $10^{12}$.[^6]

Mass is energy. **\[Argued\]** Every change in a body’s energy changes its mass by $\Delta m = \Delta E/c^2$:

- **Heat.** Warming a kilogram of water by 100 degrees adds 419 kilojoules and $4.7\times10^{-12}$ kilograms.

- **Binding.** A helium nucleus weighs 0.76 per cent less than the two protons and two neutrons it is made of; the difference is the energy released in binding them.

- **Motion.** A body’s energy, and its resistance to further acceleration, rise as $\gamma = 1/\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}$. Electrons at the LEP collider reached $\gamma \approx 2\times10^5$.

- **Stored energy.** A phone battery charged with 15 watt-hours gains $6\times10^{-13}$ kilograms.

- **Phase changes.** Freezing a kilogram of water releases 334 kilojoules; the ice has less mass by $3.7\times10^{-12}$ kilograms.

The conservation of mass in chemistry is the conservation of energy, at energies too small for $\Delta E/c^2$ to be weighed. Any account of mass must give all of this exactly.

#### A box of light has mass.

Light trapped between two mirrors carries energy $E$ but, bouncing back and forth, goes nowhere. The box with the light in it weighs more, by $E/c^2$, and it is harder to accelerate by the same amount. The reason is mechanical (Figure <a href="#fig:box" data-reference-type="ref" data-reference="fig:box">1</a>). Push the box, and the rear mirror moves into the light arriving at it, which strikes it shifted up in frequency and pushes harder; the front mirror moves away from the light arriving at it, which strikes shifted down and pushes less. The light resists the acceleration, and the resistance comes out exactly $m = E/c^2$. Trapped motion at $c$ behaves as mass, with inertia, from the imbalance of what it delivers to front and back. **\[Argued\]**

#### The arithmetic of the box.

Let the trapped light be two pulses, each of energy $E/2$, running opposite ways. Move the box at speed $v$, with $\beta = v/c$. The pulse running forward is shifted up in frequency by the factor $k = \sqrt{(1+\beta)/(1-\beta)}$, and the pulse running back is shifted down by $1/k$. Their total energy and momentum are then $$E_{\text{box}} = \frac{E}{2}\left(k + \frac1k\right) = \gamma E,
\qquad
p_{\text{box}} = \frac{E}{2c}\left(k - \frac1k\right) = \gamma\,\frac{E}{c^2}\,v ,
\label{eq:box}$$ with $\gamma = 1/\sqrt{1-\beta^2}$. That is exactly the energy and momentum of a body of mass $m = E/c^2$ moving at $v$. **\[Argued\]** Nothing was assumed about mass: the light had none. The factor $k$ is the same one that sets the leading and trailing exchanges of a moving body in GPT (Part 23): a moving body’s momentum is the imbalance between its front and back. Pushing on Equation <a href="#eq:box" data-reference-type="eqref" data-reference="eq:box">[eq:box]</a> gives inertia its measured shape at high speed: a body resists a push along its motion as $\gamma^3 m$ and across it as $\gamma m$.

#### The proton is almost all confined motion.

The three quarks in a proton account, by their own masses, for about one per cent of its mass. The rest is the energy of the motion and binding confined inside it.[^7] A calculation from the theory of the strong force divides the proton’s mass roughly into a tenth from the quarks’ own masses and their interaction with the vacuum, about a third from the quarks’ motion, about a third from the energy of the field that binds them, and about a quarter from a quantum effect of that field.[^8] Most of the mass of everything that can be touched is confined energy. **\[Argued\]**

#### The electron moves at $c$.

Dirac’s equation for the electron, one of the best-tested equations in physics, says that at every instant the electron’s velocity is $\pm c$. What is seen as its motion is an average over a rapid trembling, which Schrödinger named *Zitterbewegung*: about $10^{21}$ times a second over a distance near the electron’s Compton wavelength.[^9] It is too fast to observe in a free electron, but the same equation has been reproduced in trapped ions, and the trembling seen.[^10]

#### An electron cannot be made smaller.

Trying to confine an electron within less than its Compton wavelength, $2.4\times10^{-12}$ metres, does not produce a smaller electron. It produces new pairs of electrons and positrons.

#### Matter and light turn into each other.

An electron and a positron that meet become two flashes of light. Light with enough energy, passing a nucleus, becomes an electron and a positron;[^11] and in collisions of light with light alone, pairs of matter have been made from nothing but light.[^12] Matter is made of the same thing light is made of, in another form. **\[Argued\]** In GPT that thing is the medium: light is the medium’s travelling wave, and matter is its confined circulation.

#### Regularities that any account must explain.

The three charged leptons satisfy Koide’s relation, $(m_e+m_\mu+m_\tau)/(\sqrt{m_e}+\sqrt{m_\mu}+\sqrt{m_\tau})^2 = 0.666661$, against $2/3$, to within the uncertainty of the tau’s mass. **\[Argued\]** No one knows why. A weaker curiosity, noted in 1951, is that $6\pi^5 = 1836.118$, within two parts in $10^5$ of the proton-to-electron ratio; it may mean nothing. If the masses are thresholds and excited windings of knots, relations of this kind are what the geometry of the knots should produce. **\[Hypothesis\]**

# Light, Neutrinos and the Massless

Light is the medium’s travelling wave; matter is its confined circulation. **\[Hypothesis\]** A travelling wave has only a front: there is no back for it to deliver to, and no frame in which it is at rest. Its energy and momentum are related by $E = pc$, and its mass is zero. Matter has a front and a back, a frame in which it is at rest, and mass $E/c^2$. The first edition said photons are “fully aligned” with the flow and have “zero impedance”; that is restated: light is not confined.

Neutrinos have mass. **\[Argued\]** They change from one type to another as they travel, which they could not do without mass,[^20] and direct measurement puts the mass of the electron’s neutrino below about half an electronvolt, less than a millionth of the electron’s.[^21] The differences between the squares of the three masses, measured from the changes, are $7.4\times10^{-5}$ and $2.5\times10^{-3}$ eV$^2$, so the heaviest neutrino is at least 0.05 eV. What makes a neutrino’s pattern carry so little energy is open. **\[Open\]**

# Conclusion

Mass is energy, and matter is energy slowed down: motion of the medium at the speed of light, confined so that it goes nowhere. Light trapped in a box has exactly the energy and momentum of a mass $E/c^2$; the proton is almost all confined motion; Dirac’s electron already contains a loop at $c$ of radius $\hbar/2mc$, turning $2.5\times10^{20}$ times a second. The medium pressed into matter gives up its in-phase energy to the circulation and leaves as the neutral counterflow, to be re-phased far away and return: that turnover keeps matter in being, its balance gives every electron the same mass, and the inflow that supplies it is gravity. A pattern of the medium is carried by the medium, which is why every body falls alike; a fixed take per turn makes a body’s draw follow its energy, and the smallness of that take is why gravity is $10^{36}$ times weaker than charge. Every change of energy changes mass by $\Delta E/c^2$, from a charged battery to the binding of nuclei and the light of the Sun. The numbers of mass, the electron’s 0.511 MeV, the ratio 1836, the strength $1/137$, are derived by no theory yet; they are GPT’s target.

# Changes in the Second Edition

This list records what changed from the first edition and why. Changes to the argument alter what the paper claims or how it supports a claim; clarifications improve wording, sources or presentation and leave the argument as it was.

## Changes to the argument

- Mass is defined as the energy of a confined circulation of the medium at $c$, divided by $c^2$, in place of an impedance profile $\rho_{\text{imp}} = f(\text{coherence, geometry, phase})$. The evidence that matter is confined motion is added: the box of light, the proton’s mass, Dirac’s electron, pairs from confinement, matter made from light.

- The scale of that motion is set out: the electron’s loop, frequency, acceleration and force, and the conclusion that rest is the most extreme motion there is.

- Inertia’s measured shape at high speed ($\gamma$ across, $\gamma^3$ along) is given. “No universal tendency to continue motion” is withdrawn; motion is kept as a relation with the medium.

- The effects of heat, binding, stress, speed and melting on mass are kept, with their directions, and given their measured size, $\Delta E/c^2$. Depth in a field is corrected: locally unchanged, reduced as seen from far away.

- “Equal matter, different mass” and mass-tuning devices are restated: mass tracks energy to about $10^{-12}$; changes beyond $\Delta E/c^2$ are bounded and open. Effective mass in crystals is added as a measured case of inertia from exchange with a surrounding medium.

- Photons are restated as the medium’s unconfined travelling wave. Neutrinos are given their measured mass. The classification table is replaced.

- The turnover is added, joining the first edition’s “perpetually refreshed” mass and its absorption of gravitons, and set out as a full cycle in three dimensions, with its return through re-phasing, its energy route, every electron’s identical mass as a balance (tested by the constancy of the proton-to-electron mass ratio), a fixed take per turn giving a body’s draw in proportion to its energy, the transparency of matter, and identity as the invariants of the pattern.

- Inertial, passive and active gravitational mass are separated. Passive mass follows from matter being made of the medium it falls in; an earlier wording of this edition, that a body is carried at a rate set by its own intake, is corrected.

- New: the numbers of mass. The arithmetic of the box of light, giving $\gamma E$ and $\gamma (E/c^2) v$; the proton’s mass budget; a table of loop radius, turns, acceleration and holding force for five particles; the match between the loop and Dirac’s trembling; the magnetic moment and its correction; mass in the hydrogen atom and the meaning of $1/137$ as the ratio of the electron’s orbital speed to its circulation; nuclear binding, with each term of the liquid-drop formula given a mechanism; mass and the stars; why gravity is weak; and a table of the numbers still to be derived, with Koide’s relation.

- The formation of knots, their three dimensions, charge, the fragments of forced dissolution, and the path from knots to atoms, drafted for this paper, are moved to Part 19, *The Pathway to Matter*. Mass is how much; matter is how it is arranged.

- The energy equation $E = \int \Delta P_g\, dV$ and the reinterpretation of redshift through variable mass are withdrawn.
